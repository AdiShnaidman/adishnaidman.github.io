Research Interests

My research focuses on understanding and controlling the behavior of large language models through their internal representations.

I’m particularly interested in identifying representation-level signals associated with harmful, unreliable, or non-robust behavior, and in understanding how these signals emerge and evolve during inference.

A central direction in my work is inference-time control: using internal model representations to characterize behavioral states and develop principled interventions that steer model behavior without retraining.

Research interests:

  • LLM safety, robustness, and behavioral reliability
  • Mechanistic interpretability and representation analysis
  • Inference-time control and activation steering
  • Model evaluation under adversarial and distribution-shift conditions
